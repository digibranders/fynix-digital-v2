# Semantic SEO Workshop: Event Teaser

A 20-second vertical teaser announcing a live online workshop. Runs as a paid
social ad. Almost no words. The visuals carry it.

**Do not summarise this document. Execute it as a shot list.**

---

## The only text allowed on screen

Nothing else. No sentences, no bullet points, no paragraphs.

- Semantic SEO
- from confusion to clarity
- Pavel Klimakov
- 5 September 2026
- 5:00 PM IST · 11:30 UTC
- 3 hours, live on Zoom

Seventeen words for the whole video. If a shot needs more words than this list
provides, the shot is wrong.

---

## The five shots

**Shot 1, five seconds. No text at all.**
Scattered points of light drifting in dark empty space. Thin lines begin to draw
between them, one after another, until the points have wired themselves into a
glowing network. A few larger points glow warm amber as hubs. This is a
knowledge graph assembling itself. It is the whole idea of the workshop in one
image, and it needs no caption.

**Shot 2, three seconds.**
The network dims and falls back to become faint texture. Over it: *Semantic SEO*,
then beneath it *from confusion to clarity*. Nothing else in frame.

**Shot 3, four seconds. No text.**
A single glowing line draws itself from left to right across dark space. It runs
almost flat for the first third, then curves upward and climbs steeply, with a
soft warm glow filling underneath it. A bright point rides the leading edge as it
draws. This is search traffic compounding.

**Shot 4, three seconds.**
A man in a light shirt speaking on a conference stage, shot against darkness. His
name appears: *Pavel Klimakov*.

**Shot 5, five seconds. Held still.**
The event details arrive one line at a time, with a thin rule drawing between the
name and the date: *5 September 2026*, then *5:00 PM IST · 11:30 UTC*, then
*3 hours, live on Zoom*. The frame holds and does not move again.

---

## Narration

Four short lines. Total, for the whole video. Silence everywhere else, and the
first five seconds are deliberately silent.

1. "Semantic SEO. From confusion to clarity."
2. "Relevance is engineered, not guessed."
3. "Three hours, live with Pavel Klimakov."
4. "The fifth of September, twenty twenty six."

Calm and certain. A senior practitioner speaking to a peer, not a presenter
selling. No enthusiasm, no exclamation marks.

---

## Look

Deep navy, almost black. Warm amber as the single accent. Off-white text.
Editorial and restrained, like a well-set magazine page.

Large calm type with generous empty space around it. Slow motion, long holds,
soft fades. Everything is unhurried.

**Never:** stock photography, laptops, handshakes, upward arrows, magnifying
glasses, tech-blue gradients, neon, glassmorphism, 3D shapes, emoji, bullet
points, slide layouts, or more than one accent colour in a frame.

---

## Hard exclusions

Never show or say:

- Any website address or URL
- Any price, currency, or discount
- Any guarantee of rankings or traffic
- Any statistic or number other than the date and time above
- Any invented quote, name, or testimonial

---

## Audience

Working SEO professionals with three to ten years of experience. They already
know the terms. Do not explain anything.
