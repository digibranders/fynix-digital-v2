## Vertical composition rules

The frame is 1080 by 1920. Platform interface eats the edges.

- Keep all type inside the middle 60 percent of the height. Top 15 percent and
  bottom 20 percent are covered by the platform's own buttons and caption.
- Type is centred horizontally and stacked, not left-aligned. This is the one
  place the brand breaks its left-aligned rule, because vertical demands it.
- Maximum 9 words per frame. If a frame needs more, cut the frame.
- Headline type is large. It should be readable on a phone at arm's length,
  roughly 90 to 130px at 1080 wide.

---

## Visual style

Editorial and restrained. A well-set magazine page, not a software advertisement.

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Deep navy ground | `#0C1E2E` |
| Cream ground | `#FBFAF8` |
| Terracotta accent | `#E9AF88` |
| Muted slate, secondary text | `#565D64` |

- Headline face: a geometric sans, medium weight, tight letter spacing.
- One phrase per frame set in an italic serif, in terracotta. Exactly one. This
  is the brand's signature and it stops working if it appears twice.
- A faint dot grid on navy frames at about 6 percent opacity is allowed. Nothing
  louder.
- Motion: type rises a few pixels and fades in over about 300 milliseconds.
  No bounce, no spin, no zoom punch, no kinetic typography.
- Cuts land on the end of each sentence. Three cuts in the whole video.

**Never use:** stock photos of laptops or handshakes, upward arrows, magnifying
glasses over browsers, tech-blue gradients, neon, glassmorphism, 3D shapes,
emoji, more than one accent colour per frame, or any fabricated Google interface.

---

## Language rules

- Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Active voice.
- No em-dashes or en-dashes anywhere. Use a full stop, a colon, or a comma.
- No exclamation marks.
- Banned words: seamless, robust, powerful, cutting-edge, game-changing,
  leverage, unlock, supercharge, revolutionise, effortlessly, dive in.
- Banned construction: "this is not just X, it is Y".

---

## Hard exclusions

Never mention or show:

- Price, currency, discounts, or any money figure
- Refund policy
- Any guarantee of rankings or traffic
- Invented statistics, testimonials, or names
- Countdown timers or false scarcity
